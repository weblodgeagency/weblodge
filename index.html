What does “white-label” mean?

White-label means we design for your agency or business, and you deliver it to your client under your own brand. Our name or branding never appears in the final files.

Will you contact my clients directly?

No. Never. We strictly work behind the scenes. We only communicate with you or your internal team—not your end clients.

How many revisions do you offer?

Revisions are included in every package. We are professionals, so you don’t need to worry about it.

Which animation styles do you specialize in?

We specialize in Lottie, motion graphics, logo animation, and 2D animation styles.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are available only if work hasn’t started. Once we begin or deliver concepts, refunds cannot be offered due to time and resources spent.

Can you match a client’s existing brand or style guide?

Yes! Share brand guidelines, fonts, color codes, or examples — and we’ll follow them closely in all designs.

Do you sign NDAs or service agreements?

Yes, we’re happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) or Service Level Agreement (SLA) upon request for confidentiality and protection.

Do you provide voiceover or sound design?

No, clients handle voiceover or sound design. For motion graphic videos, we use our own sound assets.

For WordPress services, do you offer domain and hosting as well?

No, not directly. But if you want us to handle it, we can assist — just contact us first. Domain registration and hosting costs vary by provider.

How do I get started with a project?

Simply contact us with your idea, and we’ll guide you step by step. You will receive a fully custom package based on our plan sections.
